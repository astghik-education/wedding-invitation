02 · Our Story · Մեր պատմությունը

Seven years,one quiet yes.

Some love stories arrive like thunder. Ours came softly — a shared chair, long Sundays, burned lavash and small certainties, until one evening beneath ancient columns the quiet yes became a promise spoken out loud.

— Anna & Arman

2019

The First Glance

A crowded café on Saryan Street, one free chair, and a question — “Is this seat taken?” It was. By him, ever since.

2022

Home Became a Person

A small apartment on Komitas Avenue. Borrowed furniture, burned lavash, and the certainty that anywhere together was enough.

2025

The Proposal

At dusk, beneath the columns of Garni, Arman knelt with his grandmother’s ring. Anna said yes before he finished asking.

2026

Forever

And now, the mountains. Kecharis at golden hour — and you, walking towards us.

Garden formal. We dream in soft tones — please lean into the palette. No bright pink, no heavy gold; keep it whisper-quiet.
